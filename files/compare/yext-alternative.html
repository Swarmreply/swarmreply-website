Frequently asked

Is SwarmReply a good Yext alternative for small businesses?
Yes — if you run a single or small multi-location business. SwarmReply keeps one canonical version of your business info, syncs it to the core platforms, scans Yelp, Facebook, and other key directories weekly for inconsistencies, and guides you through claiming 8 more directories. Plus the entire reputation engine — review generation, AI replies, webchat, surveys — that Yext doesn’t offer.
What happens to my listings if I cancel Yext?
This is Yext’s most-cited drawback in independent reviews: listings managed through its network can revert to previous or incorrect data after cancellation, because the network is widely reported to suppress rather than permanently fix duplicates. Listings claimed through SwarmReply’s guided flows are owned by you and survive any cancellation — ours included.
How does SwarmReply pricing compare to Yext?
Yext’s small-business plans run $199–$999 per year per location, billed annually, and focus on listings — review generation, webchat, and campaigns aren’t part of the deal. SwarmReply is $99/mo flat with listings sync and monitoring included alongside the full review platform, no annual contract.

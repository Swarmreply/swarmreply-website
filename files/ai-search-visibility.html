Questions

Frequently asked

What is AI search visibility?
AI search visibility is how often and how favorably AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini mention your business when people ask for local recommendations. As more buying decisions start with an AI question instead of a Google search, it’s becoming as important as traditional SEO rankings.
How do I show up in ChatGPT recommendations?
AI assistants synthesize public reputation signals: review volume and recency, average ratings, how you respond to customers, and consistent business information. Building a steady stream of fresh, positive reviews with thoughtful responses is the most controllable lever — and exactly what SwarmReply automates.
How does SwarmReply measure AI visibility?
SwarmReply runs weekly scans that ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini the real questions customers ask about your category and city. It records whether you’re mentioned, how you’re described, which queries you miss, and which competitors appear instead — all tracked over time in your dashboard.
Is AI visibility included in the $99/mo plan?
Yes. Weekly AI scans across all three models, your visibility score and executive summary, competitor benchmarking, local rank tracking, and grounded recommendations are all included in SwarmReply’s single $99/mo plan — no enterprise tier required.
Does it just give me a score, or tell me what to do?
Both. Every scan ends with a plain-English executive summary — what your score means, how it moved, and which competitor is being recommended most — followed by a short, prioritized list of specific actions to try that week. It’s built so a busy owner gets the takeaway in seconds, not a dashboard to decode.
Can I see which competitors AI recommends instead of me?
Yes. The AI Competitors view shows two things side by side: the nearby businesses Google lists in your category, and the businesses the AI models actually name when customers ask — with how often each one comes up. You see exactly who’s taking the spot and how close you are to catching them.
Does SwarmReply track my Google map rankings too?
Yes. Local keyword rank tracking runs in the same dashboard on the same weekly schedule as your AI scans, so you see classic local-SEO position and AI visibility together. Connect your Google Business Profile once and your location and category are detected automatically.

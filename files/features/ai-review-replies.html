Frequently asked questions

Does SwarmReply reply to reviews automatically?

Yes. SwarmReply drafts and posts a reply to every new Google review in your brand voice, usually within minutes. You can run it fully automatic, or turn on an approval queue so you sign off before anything posts.

Will the replies sound robotic?

No. Each reply references the specifics of the review — the customer's name, what they praised or raised — and follows the tone you set (warm, professional, playful). It reads like a thoughtful owner wrote it, because it's written from your business's context, not a template.

What about negative reviews?

Negative reviews are where replies matter most. SwarmReply responds with empathy, takes the conversation offline where appropriate, and never argues. You can require approval on 1–2 star reviews so a human always reviews the sensitive ones first.

Why does replying to reviews even matter?

Google has confirmed that responding to reviews improves local search ranking, and customers are far more likely to choose a business that visibly engages with feedback. Replies also signal to AI assistants like ChatGPT that your business is active and trustworthy.

Can it reply on Facebook too?

Facebook reviews are monitored in your dashboard and you can reply to them in a click on Facebook directly. Automated posting is currently Google-only, because Google is the one major platform with a true reply API.

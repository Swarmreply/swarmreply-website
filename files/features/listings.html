Frequently asked questions

Why does SwarmReply only sync a handful of listings instead of 200+?

Because only a handful actually send you customers. We push your info live to Google — where the overwhelming majority of local searches happen — and monitor Facebook and a major location-data network that feeds maps, navigation apps, and AI assistants. The other '200+' sites competitors brag about are low-traffic directories that rarely influence a real buying decision.

Isn't being on 200+ directories better for SEO?

Consistency matters far more than count. A handful of accurate, high-authority listings does more for your local ranking than 200 listings you'll never check. And many of those 200 are populated by data aggregators that 'rent' your placement — cancel, and it can revert. We focus on the listings you own and that customers actually use.

What does 'pushing my info live to Google' actually do?

Connect your Google Business Profile once, and SwarmReply keeps your name, phone, website, hours and description accurate and in sync — then re-checks daily and flags any drift. Bing imports from Google automatically, so it stays right too.

How does a behind-the-scenes data network help me?

Major location-data networks license their data to apps people use every day — maps, navigation, and even AI assistants. Keep your data consistent at the source and it propagates across that whole ecosystem. Fix it once, fix it everywhere.

What happens when my info is wrong somewhere?

SwarmReply scans your monitored directories and shows you exactly what's off — field by field, your value versus what they list — with a direct link to fix it. No guessing, no hunting.

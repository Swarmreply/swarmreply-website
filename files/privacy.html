Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 15, 2026
SwarmReply LLC · hello@swarmreply.com

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Who We Are

SwarmReply LLC ("SwarmReply," "we," "us," or "our") operates the SwarmReply reputation management platform accessible at swarmreply.com (the "Service"). We are the data controller for information collected through our website and platform.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: hello@swarmreply.com

2.3 Information from Third-Party Platforms

When you connect third-party accounts, we receive information from those platforms necessary to provide the Services. Depending on the integration, this may include review data, business listing details, posting permissions, transaction data, appointment records, and customer contact information. The specific integrations we support fall into categories such as business listing and review platforms, social media networks, scheduling and booking tools, customer and commerce platforms, and payment processors, and may change over time. All such integrations are governed by your agreements with those platforms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Generate AI-powered review reply suggestions on your behalf
  • Send review request emails and SMS messages to your customers at your direction
  • Query third-party AI language models with your business name and category to monitor your visibility in AI-generated search and assistant results — no personal customer data is included in these queries
  • Send NPS and post-visit survey invitations on your behalf
  • Sync your business listing data to connected platforms
  • Publish and schedule social media and business profile posts on your behalf
  • Track your local search rankings for the keywords you choose
  • Operate the website chat widget and AI agent, and route conversations to your inbox
  • Generate reputation analytics, sentiment analysis, and reports
  • Send you transactional emails (account confirmations, payment receipts, alerts)
  • Send product updates and marketing communications (with opt-out available)
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Improve and develop the Services

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, we process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance: To provide the Services you have subscribed to
  • Legitimate interests: To improve our Services, prevent fraud, and communicate with you about relevant updates
  • Legal obligation: To comply with applicable laws
  • Consent: For marketing communications (you may withdraw consent at any time)

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

5.1 Service Providers

We use trusted third-party service providers who process data on our behalf, under contractual data-protection obligations, to operate the Services. These fall into categories including payment processing, cloud hosting and infrastructure, email and SMS delivery, AI content generation and analysis, search-ranking data, contact-form processing, and product analytics. These providers may only use your data to perform services for us. A current list of our service providers is available on request at hello@swarmreply.com.

5.2 Third-Party Platforms

When you connect third-party accounts (e.g., Google Business Profile), we share data with those platforms as necessary to provide the Services.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of SwarmReply, our customers, or others.

5.4 Business Transfers

If SwarmReply is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice before your information is transferred.

5.5 Mobile Information and SMS Consent

When phone numbers are used to send SMS or text messages through the Service — for example, review requests and surveys sent at your direction to your customers — no mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in or consent data, is shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We share this information only with the service providers that help us deliver the messages (such as our SMS delivery provider), and those providers are contractually prohibited from using it for any purpose other than providing the Service to us. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Recipients can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. Following account cancellation:

  • Account data is retained while your account is active, and kept after cancellation so you can reactivate — you can request permanent deletion at any time by contacting us
  • Payment records are retained for 7 years for legal and tax compliance
  • Log data is retained for 90 days
  • You may request earlier deletion (see Section 7)

8. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential cookies: Required for login sessions and security
  • Preference cookies: Remember your dashboard settings and preferences
  • Analytics cookies: Understand how the Service is used (aggregate, anonymised)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to use the Services.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive data at rest
  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel
  • Regular security reviews and monitoring

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you become aware of a security vulnerability affecting the Services, please contact us immediately at hello@swarmreply.com.

10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11. International Data Transfers

SwarmReply operates in the United States. If you are located outside the US, your information may be transferred to and processed in the US. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for any international transfer in accordance with applicable data protection law.

12. Your Customers' Data

When you use SwarmReply to collect or process data about your customers (e.g., sending review requests or surveys) or about visitors who message you through the webchat widget on your website, you are the data controller for that information and SwarmReply acts as a data processor on your behalf. You are responsible for ensuring you have appropriate legal basis and notices in place to share your customers' and visitors' data with SwarmReply and to send them communications through the Service.

13. HIPAA Notice

SwarmReply offers configurable modes intended to support healthcare providers in avoiding disclosure of protected health information (PHI) in review replies and communications. If you are a covered entity under HIPAA, you are responsible for ensuring your use of the Services is compliant with HIPAA requirements. Please contact us at hello@swarmreply.com to discuss a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) if required.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or a notice within the Services at least 14 days before they take effect. Your continued use of the Services after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
